RecFlow Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 28, 2026

✓ No data collection. Everything stays on your device.

Overview

RecFlow is a browser extension that lets you record your screen, browser tab, or camera. RecFlow does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data or recordings. All data is stored locally on your device and never leaves your browser.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect names, email addresses, or any personal identifiers.
  • We do not upload recordings or video data to any server.
  • We do not use analytics, crash reporting, or tracking services.
  • We do not use cookies or any form of user profiling.
  • We do not communicate with any external server or remote endpoint.

Local Storage

RecFlow stores the following data locally in your browser only using chrome.storage.local and IndexedDB:

  • Recordings — video files you create are saved in your browser's IndexedDB.
  • Preferences — your chosen recording mode, audio setting, and UI preferences.
  • Active recording state — temporary session data used to support pause/resume and the floating control bar. Cleared when recording ends.

This data never leaves your browser. You can delete it at any time by removing recordings from the extension or uninstalling RecFlow.

Permissions

RecFlow requests the following Chrome permissions:

  • storage — to save your preferences and recording metadata locally.
  • tabs — to open the recorder, recordings library, and floating control window in new tabs/windows, and to send control messages (pause, stop) to the active recorder tab.
  • windows — to display a floating control bar outside the browser tab during an active recording.

None of these permissions are used to access, read, or transmit your browsing data.

Screen & Camera Access

When you start a recording, the browser asks you to grant screen or camera access via the standard browser media permissions dialog. This access is used solely to capture video during that recording session. RecFlow does not retain, upload, or transmit this stream — it is written directly to local storage on your device.

Third-Party Services

RecFlow uses no third-party services, SDKs, analytics libraries, or remote APIs of any kind.

Children's Privacy

RecFlow is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL with a new "Last updated" date. Continued use of RecFlow after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
