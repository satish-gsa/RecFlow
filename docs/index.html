Features

Everything you need to record

Built for developers, designers, support teams, and anyone who needs to capture their screen quickly.

4 Recording Modes

Record your full screen, a specific browser tab, just your camera, or a screen + camera picture-in-picture mix.

Flexible Audio

Choose microphone, system audio, both combined, or record silently. Full control over your audio source.

Pause & Resume

Pause at any moment and resume without losing your recording. Perfect for longer, multi-step recordings.

Auto-save & Crash Recovery

Recordings are auto-saved every 5 seconds to your browser's local storage. Never lose a recording if your tab crashes.

Floating Controls

A compact floating control bar appears during recording so you can pause, stop, or discard without switching tabs.

100% Private

No uploads. No cloud. No account. Your recordings never leave your browser — stored only in your IndexedDB.
